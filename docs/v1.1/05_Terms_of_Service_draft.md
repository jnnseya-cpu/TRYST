**Note to counsel.** This draft is written by the product team to be reviewed, not approved. Clauses carrying the highest legal risk are marked **REVIEW** with a specific question. Three areas need particular attention:
- **clause 15** — retention of exclusion identifiers after account deletion: the lawful-basis question;
- **clause 16** — the subscription regime, whose commencement date is unsettled;

## 2. Definitions

2.1 **Member** — a person with a TRYST account. **Couple Profile** — a profile representing two Members who have each independently confirmed the link. **Keys** — the prepaid credits described in clause 16. **Intent** — an outbound expression of interest. **Brief** — the agent-generated summary described in clause 7. **Exclusion Identifier** — the irreversible identifier described in clause 15.

## 3. Who can use TRYST

3.1 You must be 18 or over. We verify this using age-assurance methods that meet the "highly effective" standard under the Online Safety Act 2023. If we cannot verify that you are an adult, you cannot use TRYST, and there is no appeal against that.

3.2 You must have the legal capacity to enter this contract, and you must not be prohibited from using a service of this kind by any court order, licence condition or notification requirement.

3.3 TRYST is available only in the countries listed at [URL]. We block access elsewhere, including in countries where using a service of this kind could expose you to criminal liability. You must not attempt to circumvent that block.

3.4 You may hold one account. If you previously held an account that we permanently closed under clause 17, you may not open another.

3.5 **[v1.1]** When you verify your age, we ask you to declare that you have no unspent convictions for sexual or violent offences, and that you are not subject to a Sexual Harm Prevention Order, a restraining order or a non-molestation order. We record that declaration separately. A declaration you knew to be false is grounds for permanent removal under clause 17.3.

## 4. What TRYST is

4.1 TRYST is an introduction and discovery platform. We provide software that lets adults find one another, communicate, and decide for themselves whether to meet.

4.2 We are not a matchmaking agency, an introduction agency acting on your behalf, an escort or companionship service, or a party to anything you arrange with another Member. We do not act as your agent and we owe you no duty to find you anyone.

4.3 We do not select, endorse, recommend, approve or vouch for any Member. Our software ranks and suggests profiles; that is a computational output, not a recommendation from us that a person is suitable, honest or safe.

> **IN PLAIN ENGLISH** — We introduce people. What happens after that is between you and them. Nothing we show you is us telling you that someone is a good idea.

## 5. What we verify — and what we do not

5.1 We do verify, before a Member can send an Intent or exchange media: that they are an adult; that they are a real, living person, by liveness check; that their photographs are of them; and, for a Couple Profile, that both partners have independently confirmed the link from their own separate devices.

5.2 We do not verify, and cannot verify:
- your relationship or marital status;
- whether a partner of yours knows about, or consents to, your use of TRYST;
- your criminal record, or whether you are subject to any order or notification requirement;
- your employment, income, health or personal circumstances;
- the truth of anything you or any other Member says.

5.3 There is no lawful route by which a UK platform can check a Member against the sex offenders register or obtain a criminal-record check on them. We do not claim to do so, and you should treat any platform that claims otherwise with caution.

5.4 Membership of TRYST is not, and must never be read as, a statement by us that a Member is safe, honest, available, unattached, or suitable for you.

> **IN PLAIN ENGLISH** — We check you are a real adult who looks like your photos. We do not check your marriage, your record, or your honesty. No UK app can check a criminal record on you — and we will never pretend we have.

> **REVIEW** — Clause 5 is drafted to anchor the whole liability structure and to keep us clear of the CPUT Regulations and the DMCC Act consumer regime on misleading practices. Please confirm the wording is strong enough to defeat an argument that our verification badges, ranking or marketing create an implied representation of safety.

## 6. Your account

6.1 Everything you tell us about yourself must be accurate, and your photographs must be current and of you. Profiles that misrepresent who you are will be removed.

6.2 Keep your access credentials, PIN and device secure. Tell us immediately if you think someone else has access to your account.

6.3 You are responsible for everything done through your account, unless someone gained access through our failure to take reasonable care.

6.4 You may not sell, transfer, rent or share your account.

## 7. Couple Profiles, and our agent features

7.1 A Couple Profile requires both partners to hold their own verified accounts and each to confirm the link from their own device. We will not create a Couple Profile on one person's say-so. Either partner may dissolve the link at any time, and we will act on that immediately without telling the other partner why.

7.2 Some features use automated agents. Before you and another Member speak, our agents may exchange structured information about intentions, boundaries, availability and verification, and produce a Brief for each of you.

7.3 Our agents never pose as you, and never speak to another Member as if they were you. Every Brief is labelled as automatically generated. A Brief is information to help you decide; it is not advice, and it is not a judgement about whether someone is safe. **[v1.1]** Where you use a paid agent feature that suggests people to contact, nothing is sent until you approve it.

7.4 A conversation only opens when both of you have seen your Brief and accepted.

> **IN PLAIN ENGLISH** — Our software does the awkward pre-screening so you don't waste weeks. It never pretends to be you, and it never tells you someone is safe.

## 8. Community Rules — what you must not do

You must not, on TRYST or in any contact that begins on TRYST:
- be under 18, or attempt to contact anyone you believe or suspect is under 18;
- share, request, or threaten to share intimate images of anyone without their consent;
- pressure anyone for images, contact, money, or sexual activity, or continue after someone has said no;
- threaten, harass, stalk, intimidate, or attempt to coerce or control another person;
- misrepresent your identity, age, or appearance, or use another person's images;
- offer or seek sexual services in exchange for money or anything of value;
- attempt to identify, locate, contact, or expose a Member outside TRYST without their agreement, or disclose that someone is a Member to anyone;
- solicit money, investment, or financial information;
- record, screenshot or copy another Member's private content or messages;
- use TRYST for any commercial, promotional or research purpose;
- scrape, reverse-engineer, or automate access to TRYST;
- evade a suspension or removal.

8.1 Exposing a Member's use of TRYST to a third party is one of the most serious things you can do on this platform, and we treat it as grounds for immediate permanent removal.

9.3 You accept that other Members may misrepresent themselves despite our checks, and you agree to take reasonable care of your own safety, including using the safety features in clause 11.

## 10. Consequences outside TRYST

10.1 We are not responsible for the effect of your use of TRYST on your relationships, marriage, family, employment, finances or reputation. Those consequences are yours.

10.2 No person who is not a party to this contract acquires any right under it. The Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 does not apply.

> **REVIEW** — Clause 10.2 is intended to head off a claim by a Member's spouse or partner. Please advise whether it is sufficient, and whether any free-standing duty could arise in tort notwithstanding it.

## 11. Safety features — and their limits

11.1 We provide safety features including Safe Meet guidance, Share My Plan (which privately tells a contact of your choosing where you are going), a check-in timer, in-app reporting, blocking, and a Panic control that contacts emergency services.

11.2 These features are aids. They depend on your device, your network, and third parties we do not control. We cannot guarantee that any of them will work at the moment you need it, and you must not rely on any of them as your only safeguard. If you are in danger, call 999.

11.3 Using a safety feature does not make a meeting safe, and choosing not to use one does not make what happens to you your fault.

> **REVIEW** — Clause 11.2 is a statement about technical limits, not an exclusion of liability for negligent design or operation of the features themselves — which we accept we cannot exclude under CRA 2015 s.65 where personal injury results. Please confirm the drafting does not stray across that line, since a term that did would be void and would jeopardise clause 21.

## 12. Your content

12.1 You keep ownership of everything you upload. You give us a limited, non-exclusive licence to host, store, encrypt, transmit and display your content solely to operate TRYST for you. That licence ends when your content is deleted, save as set out in clause 15.

12.2 Your private messages are end-to-end encrypted. We relay them; we cannot read them. This means we cannot recover them for you, and it also means we cannot moderate their content directly — we rely on your reports and on signals available to us outside the message content.

12.3 Media you send is encrypted on your device. You can revoke access at any time, which makes it permanently unreadable to the recipient. We cannot stop someone photographing their own screen with another device, and you should assume anything you send may be kept.

> **IN PLAIN ENGLISH** — We genuinely cannot read your messages. That protects you, and it also means we can only act on what you report. Assume any photo you send could be kept, whatever our software does.

## 13. Intimate images

13.1 Sharing or threatening to share an intimate image of someone without their consent is a criminal offence. On TRYST it results in immediate permanent removal, preservation of evidence, and referral to the police.

13.2 If an intimate image of you has been shared without your consent, report it to us. We will act within the time stated in our Safety Policy, support you in submitting a hash to StopNCII where you wish, and preserve evidence for any investigation.

## 14. Your personal data

14.1 Information about your sex life and sexual orientation is special category data under the UK GDPR. We process it only on your explicit consent, which you give separately from accepting these terms, and which you can withdraw at any time.

14.2 Our Privacy Notice explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your rights. It is not a summary — it is the operative document, and it prevails over anything in these terms about personal data.

14.3 We do not sell your data, we do not show advertising, and we do not embed third-party tracking in our apps.

## 15. Keeping and deleting your information

15.1 You can delete your account at any time. When you do, we destroy the encryption key that protects your data, which makes it permanently unreadable. This happens in under a minute and cannot be reversed. We cannot restore a deleted account.

15.2 Messages are deleted after 30 days by default, or after 7 or 90 days if you choose. Media **[v1.1] that you send in a conversation** is deleted after 7 days by default. **[v1.1] Your profile photos stay until you remove them or delete your account.**

15.3 There is one exception, and we want you to read it. If we permanently remove you because your conduct endangered another person, we keep a small set of Exclusion Identifiers — irreversible one-way codes derived from your identity document, device and payment method. We cannot reverse them into your personal details. We keep them so that you cannot open another account, and we keep them after your account is deleted. We do this to protect other people. We keep no evidence, no messages and no profile alongside them — only the codes, a category of reason, and the date. **[v1.1]** To make this possible, we create these codes when you verify and keep them protected with the rest of your account. If you delete your account and have not been removed under clause 17.3, they are destroyed with everything else.

15.4 If you are removed on that basis you may appeal under clause 18, and if your appeal succeeds we destroy the Exclusion Identifiers.

> **IN PLAIN ENGLISH** — Delete your account and it is genuinely gone. The one thing that survives is a code that stops a removed predator coming straight back under a new name. If that is you and we got it wrong, appeal and we delete it.

> **REVIEW** — This is the highest-risk clause in the document. We need: (a) the specific DPA 2018 Sch. 1 condition relied on — we have assumed Pt. 2 para. 18 (safeguarding of individuals at risk) — confirmed in writing; (b) a view on whether retention after erasure is defensible against an Art. 17 request; (c) confirmation that the disclosure is prominent enough, given that the rest of clause 15 promises complete erasure. We have deliberately refused to bury this. Please tell us if it needs to be more prominent still. **[v1.1]** See also Q13 on creating the codes for every verified member.

## 16. Paying for TRYST

16.1 Basic membership is free. Replying to any Member is always free. Paid tiers and Keys are described at [URL], with prices shown before you pay.

16.2 **Billing discretion.** Your card or bank statement will show a neutral descriptor that does not name TRYST. You can also pay using prepaid vouchers bought elsewhere. We do this because your privacy matters, not to obscure what you are paying for — the descriptor includes a customer service address.

16.3 **Cancelling.** You can cancel a subscription at any time, from inside the app or website, in one step. We will not ask you to call us, email us, or answer questions to cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid period.

16.4 **Your 14-day right to cancel.** Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you normally have 14 days to cancel a distance contract. If you ask us to start the service immediately, you may lose that right for digital content, or owe us a proportionate amount for services already supplied. We tell you this clearly at checkout and ask you to confirm it separately.

16.5 **Reminders.** We email you before any renewal and before any trial converts to a paid subscription. The reminder says what you will be charged, when, and how to stop it.

16.6 **Keys.** Keys spent on an Intent are refunded in full if the Member replies. Unused Keys do not expire while your account is open. If we permanently remove you, unused Keys are refunded unless removal was on a ground in clause 17.3.

16.7 **Refunds.** Where we owe you a refund we pay it to the original payment method within 14 days.

> **REVIEW** — The DMCC Act 2024 subscription regime is not yet in force, and its commencement date has moved repeatedly. We have drafted to comply with the full regime now, on the view that pre-contract information, reminder notices, cooling-off notices, end-of-contract notices and one-step cancellation are things we want anyway, and that retrofitting a payments flow is expensive. Please confirm the current commencement position and whether clause 16 already meets it, particularly on the form and prominence of the notices. Note CMA fining powers of up to 10% of global turnover.

## 17. Suspension and removal

17.1 We may warn you, limit your account, suspend it, or remove you permanently if you break clause 8, these terms, or the law, or if we reasonably believe you present a risk to another person.

17.2 We will tell you what ground we acted on, unless telling you would put someone at risk or prejudice an investigation. A permanent removal is always decided by a named person at TRYST, never by software alone.

17.3 Immediate permanent removal, with Exclusion Identifiers retained under clause 15.3, applies where:
- you are under 18, or sought contact with someone you believed to be under 18;
- you shared or threatened to share intimate images without consent;
- you coerced, threatened or attempted to control another person;
- you exposed a Member's use of TRYST;
- you are subject to a relevant court order; or
- you knowingly gave a false declaration under clause **[v1.1] 3.5**.

17.4 We may preserve evidence relating to a removal beyond the periods in clause 15, where we need it for an investigation, an appeal, or a legal claim.

17.5 Reporting another Member falsely, or organising others to report someone, is itself grounds for removal.

20.2 We publish what we disclose, in aggregate, in our transparency report.

> **IN PLAIN ENGLISH** — If someone's life is at risk, safety wins over discretion. Every time.

22.2 Your statutory rights as a consumer are not affected by these terms. Agreeing to these terms does not mean you have accepted any risk.

> **REVIEW** — Clause 22 is deliberately included and deliberately broad. We understand it removes any argument that we tried to exclude what CRA 2015 s.65 bars, and that s.65(2) means a "use at your own risk" framing would buy us nothing anyway. Our working assumption is that a narrow, honestly carved limitation survives the s.62 fairness test where an all-encompassing one would be struck out in full, taking clause 21 with it. Please confirm — and please push back if you think clause 21.3's cap is too low to be reasonable given what Members pay.
