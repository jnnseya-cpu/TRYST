Showing interest shouldn't feel like a gamble. On TRYST it's a quiet, private signal, and a "no" never lands on you.

## The problem

On many apps, popular members are drowned in messages and everyone else hears nothing back. Senders see "seen" and silence. And on some services you pay to start every conversation, whether or not anyone replies.

## How it works

On any introduction, tap **"I'd like to meet"**. That sends an **intent**: a private signal, not a message.

- The other member sees it under "Would like to meet you", with your card and what you share.
- If they say yes, you're both taken to [the handshake](/blog/the-handshake/), where each of you decides with a short brief.
- If they say no thanks, **you're never told**. Your intent simply stays "sent".
- If you both sent intents to each other, it's mutual straight away.

To keep things fair, each member receives at most **12 new intents a day**. Anything beyond that waits its turn and is delivered later, and the sender never knows it was queued.

Verified membership includes **three intents a week**. [TRYST+](/blog/tryst-plus-honest-pricing/) makes them unlimited. Replying is always free.

## Why it's different

- **No visible rejection.** A decline is never shown to the sender, so saying yes costs nothing emotionally.
- **No flooding.** The daily cap protects the people most in demand, so they can actually read what they receive.
- **Free to reply, always.** You never pay to answer someone.

## What it doesn't do

- An intent isn't a message: you can't write to someone until you've both accepted the introduction.
- The daily cap means a reply can take a little longer when someone is in demand.
- We don't tell you why someone didn't reply. Nobody owes a reason.

## In short

A private yes, an invisible no, and fair limits for everyone.
