Meeting someone new should come with a plan for when things don't go as hoped. TRYST builds that plan into the meet itself.

## The problem

The usual advice is "tell a friend where you're going". That depends on the friend paying attention at the right time, and it means telling someone about a meeting you might want to keep private.

## How it works

1. **Propose a time window** in your conversation. The other person confirms. The place and any door details stay inside your [encrypted messages](/blog/end-to-end-encrypted-messages/); TRYST never receives the venue.
2. **Turn on check-ins** for that meet: every 15 minutes to 3 hours, with 10 minutes' grace.
3. **Choose two PINs:** your check-in PIN, and a different **duress PIN**.
4. **Choose who's told** if you miss a check-in: a trusted contact, by email, for this meet only.

If you miss a check-in, your trusted contact is alerted and asked to call you. Entering the **duress PIN gives exactly the same reply as a normal check-in**, so anyone looking over your shoulder sees nothing unusual, but your trusted contact is told at once, with a message asking them to call you now and to call 999 if they can't reach you. When you're done, end the meet with **"home safe"**. Your trusted contact's details are deleted when you end the meet with your check-in PIN; until then they're kept encrypted, because the check-ins keep running and someone keeps looking for you.

**Panic** is a separate control, available at any time. It shows you how to call 999, including how to ask for help if you can't speak, and alerts your trusted contact with the same urgent message.

## Why it's different

The **duress PIN** is built for the moment when you can't openly ask for help. The **timers run on our servers**, not your phone, so a dead battery or a phone taken from you still triggers the alert. Your trusted contact learns only your alias and the time window, not who you're meeting.

## What it doesn't do

- It isn't an emergency service. If you're in danger, call 999.
- Your trusted contact can only act on what they know: your alias, the time window and anything you've told them yourself.
- At launch, trusted contacts are reached by email, not text message.
- There is no staffed safety line at launch: your trusted contact is the person we alert.

## In short

A plan for the evening, and a quiet way to ask the person you chose for help.
