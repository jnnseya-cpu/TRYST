On TRYST, you only ever see people who have proved two things: that they are a real person, and that they are an adult. Here's exactly how, and exactly what we keep.

## The problem

Discreet-connection sites have long been known for fake profiles, bots and people who aren't who they say they are. The usual fix, asking members to upload a passport, creates a new problem: a database of ID documents that becomes a target.

## How it works

Verification has three steps, done once:

1. **Face check.** A short live check with our verification provider that you're a real person present at the camera, matched to your photos.
2. **Conduct declaration.** You declare that you have no unspent convictions for sexual or violent offences, and that you aren't subject to a Sexual Harm Prevention Order, a restraining order or a non-molestation order. A declaration you knew to be false is grounds for permanent removal.
3. **ID check.** Your passport or driving licence, checked by the provider.

From the ID check, TRYST receives only: that you're over 18, your birth month and year (so your profile can show your age), and your document number, which we turn into a one-way code the moment it arrives and then discard. We then **check your age ourselves** as well. If you're under 18, the account is refused and signed out everywhere.

Until all three are done, you can build your profile, but nobody can see you and you can't see anyone.

## Why it's different

Many services make verification an optional badge. On TRYST it's the **price of entry for everyone**: no unverified profiles to wade through, and no "verified" tier you have to pay for. And because we keep a one-way code instead of your document, there is no store of passports or licences on our side to leak.

## What it doesn't do

- Verification confirms **identity and age**. It doesn't tell you how someone will behave, and it isn't a criminal record check. Use the [meet safety tools](/blog/meet-check-ins-and-duress-pin/) and trust your judgement.
- We rely on the declaration for convictions and orders; we can't see court records.
- Checks run only while our provider holds a current certificate for the method used. If it doesn't, we pause checks rather than lower the bar.
