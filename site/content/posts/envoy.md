Some members would rather be shown who to approach than work through a list. ENVOY is built for them, with one firm rule: it never acts on your behalf without your say-so.

## The problem

"Assistant" features on other services can message people automatically, so you never quite know what has been said in your name, or to whom.

## How it works

- ENVOY looks at your daily introductions and **proposes up to three people** worth contacting, with **plain reasons**: what you share and why it might work.
- It never proposes someone you've already contacted, and never one of the "something different" cards.
- **Nothing is sent until you approve each proposal**, one tap per person. Then the normal rules apply: the [private intent](/blog/private-intents/), the [handshake](/blog/the-handshake/) and every privacy protection.

## Why it's different

Every outbound intent needs **a person's approval**: yours. ENVOY narrows the choice and explains its reasoning, but the decision, and the first contact, are always yours.

## What it doesn't do

- It's built but **not available yet**, and can't be bought. We'll say so here, and in the app, the day it is.
- It never writes or sends messages.
- It doesn't see your conversations, only your introductions.

## In short

Suggestions with reasons, and a tap from you before anything happens.
