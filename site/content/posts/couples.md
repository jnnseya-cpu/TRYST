Couples looking for a third, or for another couple, run into the same problem everywhere: half the "couples" aren't real, and one partner often has more say than the other.

## The problem

Fake couple profiles run by one person are common on other platforms. Even genuine ones are usually a single account that one partner controls, so the other partner's consent is assumed rather than given.

## How it works

- **Two verified accounts.** Each partner verifies separately, with their own ID.
- **Linked from two different devices.** One partner creates an invitation; the other confirms on **their own** device within 24 hours. A link confirmed from the inviting device is refused, and each partner must be a separately verified member.
- **Approve the exact answers.** The partner confirming sees what the other has said they're looking for, and approves that exact version. If it changes in the meantime, they're asked to look again.
- **Two voices on every introduction.** Both partners see each introduction's brief and decide. With the veto set to **"either"**, one yes is enough unless the other says no; with **"both"**, you both have to say yes. Changing the veto rule needs **both** of you to ask for it.
- **Either partner can end the link at once**, without a reason. The other is told only that it ended.
- **Reconfirm every 90 days**, each with your own sign-in. If either of you hasn't, you leave introductions 14 days later until you both have.

Partners are never introduced to each other, and joining alone always remains possible without involving a partner.

## Why it's different

It's **verified consent from both people**, on both devices, for the link and for every introduction, plus an exit either of you can use instantly. That makes "couple" on TRYST mean what it says.

## What it doesn't do

- There's no shared joint profile yet: each partner keeps their own, linked together.
- It doesn't share private messages between partners. Each person's conversations are their own.
- It can't settle disagreements between you. It just makes sure both voices count.

## In short

Two real people, two devices, two votes, and one tap to leave.
