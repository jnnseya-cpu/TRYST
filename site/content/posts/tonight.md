Sometimes you don't want a plan for next week. You want to know who's free *tonight*. That's what Tonight is for.

## The problem

"Available now" features on other apps tend to broadcast you to everyone nearby, often with a precise distance, for as long as you forget to switch them off. That's the opposite of discreet.

## How it works

- **Go free for 1 to 6 hours**, within about 5, 10, 25 or 50 km.
- **Only people who are also free tonight can see you**, and only if you're within both people's chosen distance. If you're not free, you see nobody and nobody sees you.
- Each person appears with their name, age, area and an **approximate** distance band, never an exact location.
- Tap **Interested** and it stays private: they only find out if they're interested in you too. You have **10 interests per evening**.
- When it's mutual, you're both told, and a private conversation opens.
- **It switches itself off** when your hours run out, or the moment you tap Stop.

All the same protections as the rest of TRYST apply: blocks, [hidden areas](/blog/hidden-areas/), the [partner shield](/blog/partner-shield/), verification levels and your [Article 9 consent](/blog/consent-you-can-see/). Photos you've chosen to blur are never sent to anyone.

## Why it's different

Tonight is **reciprocal by design**: you can only look if you can be seen, and only by people in the same position. It's time-boxed, so you can't be left visible by accident. Interest is private until it's mutual. You get spontaneity without broadcasting yourself to a neighbourhood.

## What it doesn't do

- It doesn't share your live location. Distance is worked out from the areas you each chose, and shown only as a band.
- It can't make anyone free. On a quiet evening, the list can be empty.
- It doesn't arrange the meeting. Use the [meet tools](/blog/meet-check-ins-and-duress-pin/) once you've agreed a time.

## In short

Free for a few hours, visible only to people who are free too, then gone.
