Photos give away more than faces: where they were taken, sometimes exactly. And a profile full of borrowed or generated images is a profile you can't trust.

## The problem

Phone photos carry hidden data, often including GPS coordinates. Many services strip it only after upload, or not at all. Meanwhile, fake profiles built from stolen or computer-generated images are common.

## How it works

- **Location is stripped on your device**, before upload. Then the server re-encodes every photo, dropping any remaining hidden data.
- **Computer-generated images are refused**, and every accepted photo passes our review rules. Refused photos are never stored.
- **Your main photo** must meet the main-photo rules, because it's what other members see first.
- **Up to ten photos**, in the order you choose.
- **Blur any photo except your main one.** Blurred photos are never sent to other members at all, not even in blurred form.
- **See exactly what others see** with the card preview before you go live.

## Why it's different

Stripping location **before upload** means it never reaches us, rather than relying on us to delete it. Refusing computer-generated images keeps profiles real, alongside the [live face check](/blog/identity-and-age-verified/). And "blurred" on TRYST means *not sent*, not a filter someone can remove.

## What it doesn't do

- Anyone who can see a photo can photograph their screen. Share only what you're comfortable sharing.
- Removing location data can't remove what's visible in the picture itself, such as a street sign or your front door.
- Photo review isn't instant. Some photos wait for a person to check them.

## In short

No hidden location, no generated faces, and blurred means it stays with you.
