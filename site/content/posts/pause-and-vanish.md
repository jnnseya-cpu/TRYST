Life changes. A holiday, a difficult month, a moment of doubt. You shouldn't have to delete everything to step away.

## The problem

On most apps the choice is "stay visible" or "delete your account". Deleting is permanent and loses everything; staying visible isn't always possible. And a profile switched off by mistake, or switched back on by someone else, causes its own trouble.

## How it works

- **Pause.** You leave introductions and Tonight immediately. Nobody new can see you. Your existing conversations carry on as normal.
- **Vanish.** Everything Pause does, and **every conversation is closed**. The people you were talking to see the conversation as closed; they aren't told why.
- **Come back** whenever you like. Because returning makes you visible again, it needs [both sign-in checks](/blog/step-up-and-your-devices/), so nobody else can switch you back on.

Your profile, photos, verification and settings all wait for you. Nothing is deleted.

## Why it's different

Stepping away is **instant and one tap**; stepping back is **deliberate and only by you**. That asymmetry is what discretion needs. It protects you from the most likely risk, someone else with your phone, as much as from your own second thoughts.

## What it doesn't do

- Pause doesn't close conversations. Use Vanish for that.
- Neither deletes your data. When you want to leave for good, see [download or delete for good](/blog/download-or-delete-for-good/).
- Neither recalls messages the other person already has on their device.

## In short

Out of sight in one tap. Back in view only when you say so.
