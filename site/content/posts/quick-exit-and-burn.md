Sometimes you need TRYST off the screen right now. Sometimes you need it off every device you own. Those are two different tools.

## The problem

Closing a tab leaves it in your history and one tap away from reopening. Signing out on one device leaves you signed in on the others. And in a hurry, nobody wants to hunt through menus.

## How it works

**Quick exit**

- Every page has a **Leave** button in the corner, on the app and on this journal.
- **Press Escape twice** on a keyboard and you leave instantly.
- The page hides immediately and is **replaced** by a neutral weather page, so pressing Back doesn't bring TRYST back.

**Burn**

- **Signs you out on every device** at once.
- **Wipes TRYST's data from this device**, including the keys your messages are encrypted with.
- It's never blocked by an extra check: like Panic, reporting and cancelling, Burn always works first time.

## Why it's different

Quick exit is **built into every page**, not hidden in settings, and it replaces your history rather than adding to it. Burn is one of the few actions TRYST deliberately **never** puts behind a security step, because the moment you need it is the moment you can't afford a delay.

## What it doesn't do

- Quick exit can't erase your browser history or anything your network provider can see. Use a private window if that matters.
- Burn wipes this device and ends sessions. It doesn't delete your account; for that, see [download or delete for good](/blog/download-or-delete-for-good/).
- After Burn, signing back in needs your two checks as normal.

## In short

Escape twice to leave the room. Burn to leave every room at once.
