For many members, there's one person who must never see them on TRYST. The partner shield exists for exactly that.

## The problem

"Block contacts" features usually upload your address book to the company in readable form, which is a privacy disaster of its own. Others only work if the person is already a member, which is too late.

## How it works

1. In your account, enter a partner's **email address** (TRYST sign-up is by email).
2. **Your device scrambles it** (a one-way fingerprint) before sending. TRYST never receives the contact itself.
3. Our servers scramble it again with a secret only TRYST holds, and keep only that result.
4. Every member's own contact is scrambled the same way when they join. **If the two ever match, neither of you sees the other**: not on introductions, not on Tonight, not anywhere.
5. **Nobody is told**, not you, not them.

You can shield up to **ten** contacts. Adding one is instant; clearing your shield needs [both sign-in checks again](/blog/step-up-and-your-devices/).

## Why it's different

It protects you **before** the other person joins, it works **both ways**, and it's **silent**. Your address book never leaves your phone, and what we hold can't be turned back into an email address. It's built around a hard truth of discreet connections: the person you most need to avoid may join after you.

## What it doesn't do

- It matches the exact email or number they sign up with. If they join with a different one, the shield can't know.
- It hides profiles from each other on TRYST. It can't stop anyone discovering the app on your device.
- It isn't a way to check whether someone is a member: nothing is ever revealed either way.

## In short

Name the one person who must never see you, and they won't, without us ever learning who they are.
